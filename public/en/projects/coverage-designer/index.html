NGN Coverage Designer

Designs WiFi and camera coverage over floor plans, with heat maps, quotes and PDF export.

View demo ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Problem

Support NGN's sales and installation work: simulate and plan WiFi and video-surveillance coverage over each site's floor plan.

My role

I designed and built the whole web application and deployed it.

Stack

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Vite
  • Konva
  • Node.js
  • PostgreSQL

Result

  • Application live inside ngn.co.cr.
  • Simulation engine with a log-distance path-loss model and per-material wall attenuation; WiFi heat maps and camera field-of-view cones over floor plans.
  • A client self-quoting view separated from NGN's internal technical view, with role-based access; costs and margins stay on the backend only.
  • Automatic quotes and PDF or image export.
