NORA — fashion store

A complete online store with Tilopay payments, an admin panel and its own server.

Problem

A fashion store for Costa Rica that charges online through a local payment gateway and can be managed from its own admin panel.

My role

I built the whole store: backend, database, admin panel, Tilopay payment integration and hosting on a VPS that I manage.

Stack

  • Node.js
  • Express
  • PostgreSQL
  • Prisma
  • Tilopay
  • VPS

Result

  • A working store with online payments and its own admin panel.
